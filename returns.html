Return / Replacement Policy

Returns and replacements

Return or replacement requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis, particularly for issues relating to incorrect dispatch, damaged goods or product mismatch against the approved order details.

Customers are requested to inform us promptly after receiving the shipment so that the issue can be reviewed and resolved. Final eligibility for return or replacement depends on the specific order, product condition and agreed business terms.

For custom or bulk-purchased item sets, the policy may vary based on the final commercial arrangement. We recommend confirming all business requirements before order confirmation.
