Our Craft

From Imperial Kilns to Your Table

Asian Art Crockery began in 1987 as a two-wheel studio in Sanganer, Jaipur — the historic home of blue pottery. Three generations later, our artisans still throw every piece by hand, paint cobalt motifs freehand and fire each batch in the kilns our founders built.

Our wave-and-cloud vocabulary travels from the trading ports of Jingdezhen through Mughal court ateliers — then gets sealed under a food-safe glaze built for dishwashers, dinner parties and decades.

  • Natural cobalt & mineral pigments only
  • Twice-fired, chip-resistant porcelain
  • Every piece signed by its artist
  • Food-safe, lead-free glazes

The Making

Four Fires, One Story

From wet clay to jewelled glaze — every piece passes through the same unhurried ritual.

Step 01

Wheel Thrown

Porcelain bodies shaped by hand on the wheel, exactly as in 1987.

Step 02

Bisque Fired

A first firing at 950°C strengthens each piece for the glaze.

Step 03

Freehand Painting

Clouds, waves and fret borders in cobalt — no transfers, no stencils.

Step 04

Glazed & Fired

A final 1300°C firing seals the deep, jewel-like blue forever.
