Delivery policy

Delivery timelines for bulk ceramic orders depend on product type, quantity, packaging requirements and final order confirmation. Specific dates are communicated at the time of order acceptance.

For business orders, customers can discuss delivery destinations, packing requirements and repeat shipment plans. We aim to coordinate reliable dispatch support for restaurant, hotel and corporate procurement requirements.

Any additional transport, special packaging or destination-specific logistics requirements may be communicated separately at the time of quotation.
